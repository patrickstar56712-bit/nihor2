Loot Tracker

The last log files visitors loaded in the Loot Tracker (only their loot lines; the newest 200 are kept). Open one to see it in the Loot Tracker. Logs loaded in a logged-in browser are not kept.

Dungeon Keys

Each dungeon's key next to its chest. Pick or write their names, click a picture box to upload its picture: it is saved on the website and shows in Items (as Dungeon Key / Dungeon Chest) too.

Screen Tracker
